## Title
Cliff Edge

## Short description
Hold to drive, let go to stop. Park as close to the cliff edge as you dare. One centimeter too far and you're flying.

## Full description
Cliff Edge is a one-touch driving game about nerve and timing. Hold to drive toward the edge of a cliff, then let go and slide to a stop. Stop under the target distance to clear the cliff. Go one centimeter too far and your little pink car tumbles into the sea.

Every cliff asks you to stop a little closer than the last, from under 1 meter down to under 1 centimeter.

- Endless cliffs: each target is smaller than the one before.
- 3 hearts: a miss costs one heart and you retry the same cliff.
- Checkpoints every 5 cliffs, so a bad run never sends you back to the start.
- Three ground types that change how far you slide: road, ice and mud.
- A ruler on the cliff and a ghost of your last try help you read your timing.
- Daily Challenge: the same cliff for every player each day, 3 tries, and a day streak.
- Your progress is saved on your device.

## Controls
- Desktop: hold the left mouse button, Space, Enter or the Up arrow to drive. Release to brake.
- Mobile and tablet: touch and hold the screen to drive. Release to brake.
- Tap, click or press Space after a round to play the next one.
