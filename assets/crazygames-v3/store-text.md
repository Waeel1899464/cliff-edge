## Title
Cliff Edge

## Short description
Hold to drive, let go to stop. Park as close to the cliff edge as you dare, beat five bosses across five worlds, and don't fly off!

## Full description
Cliff Edge is a one-touch driving game about nerve and timing. Hold to drive toward the edge of a cliff, then let go and slide to a stop. Stop under the target distance to clear the cliff. Go one centimeter too far and your little pink car tumbles off the edge.

Travel through 5 worlds of 10 hand-made cliffs each. Every world adds a new twist that changes how your car moves:

- Green Hills: road, ice and mud, then a road that tilts downhill.
- Desert: sand, plus wind that pushes you on or holds you back. The arrows show its direction and strength.
- Snow Mountain: the ground changes before the edge, and fog hides the edge until you get close.
- Moon: low gravity, long floaty slides and slow, funny falls.
- Volcano: the edge crumbles as you get close, and the ground shakes.

The second half of each world is a boss battle in 5 phases: the Giant Hill, the Sandstorm, the Blizzard, the Crater Monster and the Angry Volcano. Each cliff you clear lands a hit, and the last one takes the boss down.

- Stars: GOOD, GREAT or PERFECT. Collect up to 3 stars on every cliff, and replay any cliff from the world map.
- Coins: the closer you stop, the more you earn.
- 3 hearts (4 in boss battles). A checkpoint at the start and middle of every world, so you never lose a world you reached.
- Stuck? Rescues and helpers give you a second chance in the story (a few per day, free).
- Endless mode unlocks after the final boss: new cliffs mixing every twist. How many can you clear in a row?
- Daily Challenge: the same cliff for every player each day, 3 tries, and a day streak.
- A ruler, plus your last release point and last stop, help you read your timing.
- Your progress is saved on your device.

## Controls
- Desktop: hold the left mouse button, Space, Enter or the Up arrow to drive. Release to brake.
- Mobile and tablet: touch and hold the screen to drive. Release to brake.
- Tap, click or press Space after a round to play the next one.

## Notes for the submission form (not listing text)
- Orientation: portrait (9:16). On desktop the game is centered with a matching background on the sides.
- Language: English.
- No login, no in-game purchases and no ads in this build. Rescues, helpers and bonuses are free with small daily limits. Rewarded ads can be connected later through a single function (`requestReward`).
- Competitive modes (Daily Challenge, Endless) have no rescues, helpers or bonuses.
